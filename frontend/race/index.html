Shift timing: Voice cuts work from 7,000 RPM. Below 8,500: Early · 8,500–9,500: Perfect · above 9,500: Late. Holding the 10k marker for more than 3 seconds causes engine failure. Shift, brake or release the throttle to recover before then.

Driving: a short break in your rev shifts up; one small rev shifts down. When the car slows to the bottom of a gear, it shifts down by itself. Holding a gear at the redline cuts power, and more than 3 seconds at 10k blows the engine. Tap N ↔ D any time for neutral; tap again to drive in a gear that suits the speed. More than 3 seconds of silence selects neutral automatically. RPM and gears are estimates.
